# Clinical AI Copilot — Safety & Compliance Disclosures

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **INFORMATIONAL & PORTFOLIO DEMONSTRATION USE ONLY**
> "Clinical AI Copilot" is a portfolio-grade demonstration project. It is **NOT** a certified medical device (FDA/CE/CDSCO), does **NOT** provide medical diagnoses, and must **NEVER** be used to guide real patient care without independent review by a licensed healthcare professional.

---

## 1. Regulatory & Clinical Non-Diagnostic Disclosure
- **No Autonomous Clinical Action**: Every output generated by this application—whether radiology summaries, lab flagging, prescription recommendations, or OR decision support—is advisory only.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Requirement**: A licensed physician or radiologist must review, verify, and validate all AI-generated content before any medical action, order, or prescription is executed.
- **Persistent UI Warnings**: All application screens display a persistent, non-dismissible clinical disclaimer footer:
  `"AI-generated. Not a diagnosis. For informational use — a licensed clinician must review before acting."`

---

## 2. Model Inference & Local Training Policy
- **Pretrained / API Inference Only**: No machine learning model training, parameter fitting, or weight fine-tuning was performed on local machines.
- **Gemini Multimodal API**: Reasoning, multimodal document extraction, vision parsing, and transcript analysis are routed to Gemini API models (`gemini-2.5-flash`).
- **Radiology Architecture (`MriAnalyzer`)**: DICOM decoding is handled via `pydicom`. Swapping in local MONAI or TorchVision models preserves the existing input/output interface without modifying clinical pipelines.

---

## 3. Patient Data & Privacy Protection (HIPAA / DPDP)
- **Synthetic Demo Data**: No real patient records, protected health information (PHI), or actual DICOM patient studies are included. All demo patient records (Eleanor Vance, Arthur Pendelton, Maya Lin) are 100% synthetic.
- **Privacy Controls**: Zero third-party analytics scripts tracking user input. Patient records are handled strictly in-memory during demo sessions.
- **Consent Gate Protocol**: Consultation Mode enforces an explicit on-screen patient consent gate (`ConsentGateModal`) before microphone audio streaming activates.

---

## 4. Live OR Mode Scripted Demo Framing
- **No Live Video Analysis**: The "Live OR Mode" module is a text-grounded Q&A/narration demo driven **strictly by pre-written JSON case scripts** (`/backend/data/case_scripts/`).
- **Decorative Camera Feed**: The on-screen video panel is purely decorative for atmospheric context and is **not connected to any AI vision pipeline**.
- **Inline Source Tagging**: Every vital sign, visual finding, and anatomical risk warning displays an explicit inline tag: `"from case script"`.

---

## 5. Mock Pharmacy Inventory Engine
- **Simulated Stock Checks**: Pharmacy stock statuses and alternative drug recommendations generated in Consultation Mode are served from a local mock database (`PharmacyInventoryChecker`). No live EHR or retail pharmacy orders are submitted.
